Storm-day field checklist, sir. Rain gear on before you step out — jacket, pants, and waterproof boots. Mud is the real hazard: slow down on slopes and wet grass, and test your footing before you commit your weight. Drink water through the shift even in the rain — storms don't stop dehydration. If lightning is near, get off the mowers and away from trees, fences, and metal. This is advisory only, sir — the safety calls on site are yours.
